# Commercial boundary

This repository is the **open-source core** of DemoMotion AI.

The OSS core includes:

- scenario generation interfaces
- browser recording pipeline
- narration and subtitle generation hooks
- ffmpeg-based video composition
- local Docker Compose demo
- minimal Google Cloud Terraform

The commercial hosted product, **DemoMotion Cloud**, is expected to live in a separate private repository and may add:

- authentication and teams
- Stripe billing and credit ledgers
- private premium templates
- brand kits and workspace settings
- higher quality rendering presets
- queue priority and retry policies
- hosted video sharing pages
- commercial usage terms
- support, admin tools, and operational dashboards
